Effective 19 October 2026

Lumen is a Bible reading app. Scripture is always free, there are no ads, and reading does not require an account.

This site hosts the privacy policy, terms, and support information for the Lumen app. It is a static site; it does not set cookies or run analytics.

  • Privacy policy — what stays on your device, and what a future sync build would send.
  • Terms of use — the licence to use the app and the limits of the promise.
  • Support — bugs, questions, and privacy requests.

Lumen is an independent app. The KJV and WEB texts are public domain; the app never charges for scripture.
