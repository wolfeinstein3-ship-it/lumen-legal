Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · applies to the Lumen app and this site

The short version. Lumen does not require an account. Your reading and progress stay on your device. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no sale of personal data. Sync and purchases are not enabled in this build; the sections below describe what will happen if you choose them in a future build.

Who is responsible

Lumen is published by an independent developer. For privacy questions, use the support page and mark the request “Privacy”.

Data that stays on your device

In the current build the app stores the following locally, using the operating system’s secure storage (Keychain / Keystore) or the browser’s local storage:

  • the goal you choose during onboarding;
  • your reading progress: the plan day, streak, last-read date, and completed days;
  • your reminder preference and the notification schedule;
  • a cached premium-entitlement record, if the premium layer is enabled in a later build.

This information is not transmitted to us in the current build. You can export it or reset it at any time in Settings → Your data.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled by your device, not by a server. The reminder text is chosen on the device from a small set of written variants; it is not personal data sent to us. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in the operating system’s notification settings.

No advertising, analytics, or tracking

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no cross-app tracking, and no fingerprinting. We do not sell or rent personal data.

Sync and accounts (not enabled in this build)

If you choose to connect sync in a future build, we will use Supabase to store: your email address, a user identifier, and the content you choose to sync (highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading log, plans, and settings). Row-level security policies restrict every row to its owner. You will be able to export your data and delete your account in the app; deleting the account deletes the synced rows.

Purchases (not enabled in this build)

If premium purchases are enabled in a future build, they will be processed by Apple’s App Store or Google Play, with RevenueCat used to verify entitlements. We will not receive your payment-card details. The stores’ own privacy policies apply to the purchase.

Service providers

  • Expo / EAS builds and delivers the app and its updates.
  • Supabase provides sync and authentication, only if you enable sync.
  • RevenueCat verifies purchases, only if purchases are enabled.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this policy and the support pages.

These providers process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own terms. We do not authorise them to sell your data.

Retention

Local data remains until you reset it in Settings or uninstall the app. If sync is enabled, synced data remains until you delete your account, after which it is removed from the active database. Support issues remain public until resolved and can be removed on request.

Security

The app uses HTTPS for any network request. The public Supabase key is designed to be public; access is controlled by row-level security, not by hiding a key. Service-role keys are never shipped in the app.

Children

Lumen is not directed at children under 13. The current build has no accounts and collects no personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a future sync feature, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data. In the current build you can exercise the data ones directly in Settings: export produces a JSON file on your device, and reset erases the local reading state. For anything else, use the support page. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If a future build changes what is collected — for example when sync or purchases are enabled — this page will be updated before that build ships, and the effective date will change.

Lumen is a Bible reader first. The text is never the product, and your reading is not the price.
