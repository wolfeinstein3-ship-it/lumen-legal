Support

Bugs, questions, privacy requests

Lumen is a small independent app. The fastest way to reach the developer is a GitHub issue. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal data in a public issue.

Report a bug or ask a question

Open an issue with:

  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
  • your platform (iOS or Android), app version, and device model;
  • the chapter and screen you were on, if the issue is in the reader.

Screenshots help. If the issue is about a notification, include whether it was scheduled, delivered, or missed, and your time zone.

Privacy requests

For access, export, correction, or deletion of personal data, open an issue titled “Privacy request” and describe the request without including the data itself. We will arrange a private channel if we need details. In the current build there is no account: your data is on your device, and Settings → Your data lets you export or reset it.

Response time

This is a solo project, so replies usually take a few days. Security reports are prioritised; please open the issue with “Security” in the title and keep the details minimal until we can talk privately.

What this site is

This is the public support and legal site for the Lumen app. It is a static site and does not set cookies or run analytics.
